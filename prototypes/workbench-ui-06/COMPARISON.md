# Panel density model comparison

Status: **A — Elastic Triad selected during live human review**

All candidates preserve the accepted Scoped Evidence Workspace, one current Local Injection Draft, immutable Source, exact target, Local-only execution boundary, explicit Server/Local provenance, compact Back restoration, and no planned Server Injection workflow.

They disagree about how much simultaneous context survives when geometry or a demanding operation consumes the panel.

## Provisional selection

Use **A — Elastic Triad** as the baseline. It best balances evidence continuity with the accepted requirement that large raw-JSON Local Injection work must receive enough room. Stable pane identities may move or park without reconstructing scope, selection, focus, scroll, filters, Live/Frozen state, inspector lens, or a safe draft. Document-heavy work promotes to the full canvas and returns to an exact checkpoint.

The live review accepted a two-axis capacity solver. The prototype uses approximately `1120px` for Wide Triad, `700px × 440px` for Normal Stack, the same width with less height for Shallow Side, and Focused Compact otherwise. Production derives its gates from the usable content box and pane minima, with approximately `32px` of hysteresis to prevent layout flapping during DevTools resizing.

The live review also accepted distinct restoration semantics: geometry-parked panes return automatically when capacity returns, explicitly collapsed panes remain collapsed, each orientation remembers its own dimensions, and returning panes never steal focus or selection.

The live review accepted a deterministic reduction hierarchy: unpin Scope, relocate Context, focus one surface, overflow low-frequency actions, then adopt the compact evidence-row grammar. Capture state, Live/Frozen state, scope, evidence identity, textual provenance, selected identity, Local Injection safety context and action, and material diagnostics cannot be reduced away.

The live review accepted explicit scroll ownership: no shell scroll; one content scroll per visible pane; fixed structural and action bars; one primary scroll in compact/promoted surfaces; one shared Source/Draft comparison scroll; and no whole-panel horizontal scroll. Live Capture cannot move a non-following anchor.

The live review accepted compact developer-tool density: `28–30px` structural strips, a `24–32px` status/action strip, at least `24×24px` independent controls, `26–28px` normal/wide evidence rows, `40–44px` compact two-line rows, and `18–20px` noninteractive code lines. Back, current state, and the consequential action remain reachable in shallow geometry; `563×137` is emergency-operable rather than comfortable.

The live review accepted the promotion boundary: Local Injection and Source comparison always receive the full canvas; deep raw evidence, diagnostics, lifecycle analysis, and export preview/review promote when Context is insufficient; concise contextual material remains in Context. Promotion is singular, temporary, and exactly reversible.

The density decision is final. The durable contract is recorded in [`docs/WORKBENCH_PANEL_DENSITY_AND_DOCKED_LAYOUT.md`](../../docs/WORKBENCH_PANEL_DENSITY_AND_DOCKED_LAYOUT.md).

## A — Elastic Triad

Scope, Evidence, and Context are stable pane identities. Wide shows all three, normal stacks Evidence and Context, wide-but-short places them side by side, and compact parks everything except one active surface. Large document work is promoted to the full canvas.

Strongest at preserving context and user-resized panes through docking changes. Its cost is the most complex pane-state model.

## B — Anchored Context Deck

Evidence remains visible at normal and wide sizes. Selection details and tools share one bottom deck that can collapse, resize, or maximize while leaving an evidence strip. Compact explicitly replaces evidence.

Strongest at continuous correlation with incoming evidence. Its honest cost is reduced JSON-editor height, competing motion during live Capture, and two simultaneous scroll regions.

## C — Viewport Lease

Exactly one surface owns the canvas. Evidence is the root; detail, Local Injection, diagnostics, and export explicitly replace it and restore an exact checkpoint on Back. Wide space adds columns or internal comparison rather than more permanent panes.

Strongest at compact reliability, minimal chrome, and large-document room. Its cost is more explicit transitions and less ambient correlation.

## Geometry under review

- narrow/tall compact: `563×700`;
- normal: `900×700`;
- wide/short: `900×320`;
- wide: `1440×900`;
- emergency shallow evidence such as the existing `563×137` export verification remains operable, not comfortable.

The review must choose rules for pane minima, promotion, scroll ownership, resize restoration, shallow docking, and intentional information reduction—not only a preferred screenshot.

## Initial verification observations

- All three candidates fit their representative frames without panel-level horizontal or vertical overflow.
- Elastic Triad changes a `900×700` normal stack into a side split at `900×320`, confirming that height must participate in the layout solver.
- Anchored Context Deck preserves evidence during Local Injection, but its ordinary deck leaves a visibly constrained editor; maximization materially improves the editor while reducing evidence to a small retained strip. This is the model's central trade-off, not a styling defect.
- Viewport Lease gives the editor the largest uninterrupted surface and the simplest scroll ownership, while removing ambient evidence from the active canvas.
- Compact evidence uses a two-line row grammar and retains textual Server/Local provenance without panel-level overflow.
- Light and Dark geometry match. Selection has a leading marker, and provenance/status remain textual rather than color-only.
